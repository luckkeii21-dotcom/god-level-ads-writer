Trigger 01 · Certainty

We rarely buy the best option.
We buy the one we're sure about.

Uncertainty registers in the brain as risk. People resolve it by choosing whoever removes doubt fastest — a clear answer, a confident tone, a frictionless next step.

"Let me check and get back to you…"
Vague, delayed, hedged — reads as risk.
versus
"Yes — here's exactly how it works."
Specific, calm, certain — reads as safe.
