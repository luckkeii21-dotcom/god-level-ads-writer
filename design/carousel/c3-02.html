The 5 habits

Recognize any of these?

01
Pitching before you've listened
Talking at people, not with them — they tune out in seconds.
02
A weak, generic first line
"Hi, is this a good time?" hands control — and trust — straight away.
03
Filling every silence
The pause is where they think. Step on it and you lose the answer.
04
Features instead of questions
Nobody buys a spec sheet. They buy the problem getting solved.
05
No clear next step
"I'll follow up sometime" is where most deals quietly die.
